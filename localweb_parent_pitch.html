02 / Why this can work

Small businesses need
to be easy to find.

This is a testable service, not a guaranteed success. I can start with one local niche, learn what owners actually need, and improve the offer based on real conversations.

THE CUSTOMER PROBLEM

A good business can still have a poor online presence.

Potential customers often want quick answers: what does this business offer, where is it, when is it open, and how do I contact it? A clear website can make those answers easier to find and help the business look credible.

My advantage: I can build the first version myself using AI-assisted tools, so I don't need to hire a developer to test the idea.

10 / A controlled 30-day pilot

Test the idea before
making big commitments.

The first goal is not to become rich in 30 days. It is to prove that I can build something good, find real prospects and earn one satisfied customer.

WEEK 01
Build the demo

Make one polished sample site for a fictional business. Test it on mobile and desktop. Track all costs.

WEEK 02
Research 20 prospects

Choose one local niche. Record public business contact details and a specific, respectful reason each might benefit.

WEEK 03
Start conversations

Use personalised, honest outreach. Ask permission to show a concept. No spam, fake claims or pretending to be an established agency.

WEEK 04
Review the evidence

Track replies, calls, proposals, time spent and any sale. Improve the offer—or stop if the evidence doesn't support continuing.

11 / Risks & safeguards

Take the risks seriously.

I know this is not risk-free. The point of a small pilot is to keep the downside limited, make responsibilities clear and stop if the evidence or school results say to stop.

School performance drops
Schoolwork first; fixed weekly time limit; pause during exams.
No one buys
Run a 30-day test; spend very little; review evidence before scaling.
Client expects too much
Written scope, revision limits, delivery date and approval before work.
Security or payment issue
Parent involved in contracts and payments; never collect unnecessary customer data.
Website breaks
Test before launch; document ownership and backups; be honest about support limits.
Privacy or spam concerns
Use legitimate public business contact channels; personalise messages; respect opt-outs.
